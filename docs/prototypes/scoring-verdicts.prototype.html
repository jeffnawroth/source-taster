Scoring & verdict workbench

How should field agreement, source identity and contradictory evidence produce one Verdict?

All records, source identities and DOI observations are invented fixtures. No network, storage or corpus evaluation. Trial thresholds are discussion inputs, not calibrated launch values. “Decision needed” marks an unresolved design branch, not a seventh product Verdict.

Accepted rounds 1–3: Score alone does not establish identity; use supplied identifier proof, strong title + authors, or verified web URL + title evidence. Coverage comes before DOI conflict, which comes before Mixed citation. Proven provider retraction prefixes are comparison-only decorations. Expanded Not verifiable reasons and coverage precedence are approved amendment requests for the next specification version; v3.1 remains unchanged. Author assignment is one-to-one; order and unmarked omissions are deviations; explicit et al. truncation is allowed. Dates compare shared components. Pages use coverage of the cited range. Numeric settings remain uncalibrated trials.

What this experiment does — and leaves open

    

  • The specification's weights are fixed here: title 30, authors 25, date 15, journal 15, DOI 10, volume 3, pages 2. Only fields present on both sides participate; their weights are renormalized.
    • 

  • The sample text metric is normalized edit distance with adjacent transpositions. “Jouranl” versus “Journal” is 85.71%. Matching initials and surname spelling variants can agree fully; conflicting full given names cannot agree just because their initials match. Authors are assigned one-to-one. Reordering uses an illustrative 10% penalty; its size is not approved. Explicit et al. compares the supplied leading names without penalizing omitted trailing names.
    • 

  • Dates compare shared year/month/day components; a one-year difference halves year agreement. The 80/15/5 component weights remain an uncalibrated illustration. Pages use intersection length divided by the cited range length: contained pinpoints agree fully, partial overlap proportionally. Numeric Arabic ranges only; article numbers and other page formats are not validated here.
    • 

  • “Correct” needs an unambiguous match and 100% agreement in every compared field. A high total alone cannot make a deviating field disappear. Missing fields are excluded, never treated as zero.
    • 

  • The highest total determines the Best match. A stable display order does not turn equal-scoring different Sources into a unique match. The trial margin uses the next different Source identity.
    • 

  • DOI, mixed-citation, coverage and outage precedence are deliberately visible. Definitive non-existence, a foreign DOI and unavailable resolution are different observations. Missing retraction evidence is not a negative.
    • 

  • This is an offline snapshot of at most two Candidates from one synthetic Data source. It does not implement the sequential search, five-Candidate allocation, retries, source adapters, bibliographic parser or production normalization. It does not measure recall, latency or false alarms.
    • 

  • Try cases before selecting policy. Actual thresholds are calibrated only on the Development set and then checked on the separate Reference test set. Every changed rule/threshold needs a new measured Scoring rules version (ADR-0006/0007).
