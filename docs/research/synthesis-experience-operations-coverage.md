# Experience and operations coverage for the final technical concept

Research date: 25 October 2026. Authoritative baseline: **approved English requirements v3.3**, not earlier German or English versions. Source snapshot and branch base: `bae878334c3bdc3a5e29ebbdfbc070be5783251a`. Branch: `research/synthesis-experience-operations-20261025`.

This is a planning-only synthesis of local approved requirements and accepted decisions. It covers all 73 assigned numbered requirements: every FA-GLE, FA-ERG, FA-KON, FA-BTR and FA-EIN, plus FA-UEB-01–06, 10–17 and 44–48. AGENTS.md and GLOSSARY.md were read first; the predecessor repository and local copies were neither searched nor inspected. The nine assigned ADRs were read in full; relevant supporting ADR sections and issue #4 Notes were consulted. No external capability investigation, implementation, corpus work, product tests, provisioning or legal review was performed.

Every requirement ID below links to its exact v3.3 row at the source snapshot. Design citations link to exact sections at that same immutable commit. An accepted ADR records a choice; it is never a passed test. “Remaining evidence” describes what would establish acceptance, not an implementation backlog.

## Reading the statuses

- **design recorded**: a mechanism or deliberately limited preparation hook is selected. This does not mean implemented or accepted. For should/deferred rows, “hook only” expressly means the full feature remains undesigned and outside current launch scope.
- **explicit qualification gate**: the ADR expressly conditions use/release/activation on measured, contractual, deployment or browser evidence. Failure can require a new decision; it cannot silently weaken v3.3.
- **genuine design gap**: a relevant choice or interaction is not selected in the reviewed decisions. For a should/deferred feature this is an expected non-launch gap unless its specified preparation is missing.
- **candidate amendment**: an unresolved proposed change to the approved baseline affects coverage. The current requirement continues to govern until the client approves a new specification version.

[Specification §1.3][S-obligation] makes must requirements launch conditions, should requirements expansion scope and deferred requirements conditional on §19. The issue #4 Notes further limit present should work to necessary architectural hooks. An evidence cell for a non-launch feature applies when that feature is pursued; it does not promote the feature into launch.

## Gap summary for synthesis

The launch architecture records concrete mechanisms for server-owned parity, stable results/corrections, History, PDF generation, private Operator administration, recipient-only rights delivery, hosting/recovery, content-free telemetry and no launch analytics. The most consequential unresolved **design clarification** is §10 preference persistence/equality, especially Guest web/extension settings; protected selection surfaces remain an explicit acceptance-scope decision if native behavior cannot satisfy the unchanged requirement. Expansion/deferred search, themes, onboarding, About, advanced metric definitions, public links/table export and full expert/BYOK/project/retention features are not fully designed, intentionally outside current launch scope except their stated hooks/preparation.

The greatest **qualification dependencies** are the actual AI launch/reserve selection/contracts, provider location register, host-enforced parser topology and budget/capacity, exact PDF/site graphs and licenses, real browser/version coverage, actual all-surface WCAG/PDF reading evidence, complete security assessment/scans and both restore paths with deletion/current authority/payment consistency. These are not passed and not substitutes for unresolved choices. Reported-copy encryption/rights/retention and independent audits have their feature/trigger gates; they are not new launch work.

No amendment candidate changes v3.3 here. Launch still requires every must criterion, required preparation and [§16 evidence][S-acceptance]: dated/versioned both-client acceptance log; unchanged quality targets; performance/live load; accessibility; security; legal/license clearance. Record should deviations; do not treat deferred features as required implementation or omit their explicit preparation. No implementation backlog is proposed.
