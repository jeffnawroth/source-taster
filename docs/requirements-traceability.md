# Requirements and acceptance traceability

This matrix is the coverage companion to the [technical concept](technical-concept.md), based on the client-approved [requirements v3.4](requirements-specification.md). Version 3.4 changes only the FA-ERF-03 acceptance criterion against the audited v3.3; every other row was audited against unchanged wording. It inventories **200 unique numbered requirements: 118 must, 17 should, 65 deferred**. Each row names the selected mechanism or preserved future seam, its governing ADR and concrete evidence still required. The specification retains the full authoritative requirement and acceptance text; neither is replaced by the shorter wording here.

**No row is an implementation pass.** Must rows belong to launch; should rows remain expansion scope (only necessary hooks are fixed now); deferred rows require the exact section 19 trigger, client reassessment and a new specification version before implementation. Their future acceptance is distinguished from mandatory launch preparation. Q01–Q11 link to open qualification packets; an unadopted candidate in the concept is not permission to change a criterion.

The unnumbered coverage and all 24 trigger/preparation rows follow the numbered matrix. Read historical handoffs through the later accepted resolutions described in the concept.
