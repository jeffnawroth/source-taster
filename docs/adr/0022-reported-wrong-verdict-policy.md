# Keep reported wrong Verdicts purpose-bound, separately expiring and removable

The client accepted this design on 25 October 2026 in [Reported wrong-Verdict copies: purpose and retention](https://github.com/jeffnawroth/source-taster/issues/58), through [F1–F3](https://github.com/jeffnawroth/source-taster/issues/58#issuecomment-6398468101), [F4–F6](https://github.com/jeffnawroth/source-taster/issues/58#issuecomment-6398519255), [F7–F9](https://github.com/jeffnawroth/source-taster/issues/58#issuecomment-6398563001) and [F10–F11](https://github.com/jeffnawroth/source-taster/issues/58#issuecomment-6398646201). Investigate the specifically reported result and derive improvements, while retaining the submitted copy only for that purpose. This trades a reusable customer-content archive for bounded diagnosis, explicit permission and independently rebuilt development cases.

The approved baseline remains [requirements v3.3](../requirements-specification.md). FA-ERG-15 and FA-BTR-08 remain **should**: this records the existing architectural hook and its activation conditions, not a launch obligation, implemented reporting surface, collected corpus or requirements amendment. [ADR-0005](0005-check-reference-lifecycle.md)'s copy model, [ADR-0006](0006-reference-test-set-quality-gate.md)'s dataset exclusion, [ADR-0014](0014-operator-administration.md)'s content boundary and [ADR-0021](0021-online-dpa-workflow.md)'s contractual scope remain the foundations.

## Purpose, permission and one-result snapshot

Before each submission, disclose the investigation/improvement purpose, exact diagnostic scope, separate deletion deadline and removal method. Let the user inspect the actual proposed content and explicitly consent to sending **this one Reference with this result**. Without consent no reported copy or Operator access is created. History consent, DPA conclusion, general support mail and an ordinary Check are not reporting consent. Permission to report is not a determination of the lawful basis or rights for other people mentioned in the content; specialist review must qualify those separately before activation.

The immutable copy contains the original Reference input, the checked field revision, the result's Candidates, Field scores/Scores, Best match, Verdict and explanation, applicable Manual override with the system Verdict retained, relevant search steps/failures and actual scoring/search/configuration version references. Limit all diagnostic material to the selected Reference and result. Do not copy other References, the entire Check/document/bibliography, filenames or document source-span context outside this Reference. Preserve bibliographic provenance needed to understand that Reference without serializing the document provenance container or unrelated provider-response material. Disclose this projection rather than treating the word “result” as consent to an unrestricted object dump.

Bind the submission to its original Check/slot/result using opaque identifiers so deliberate deletion can reach the copy even after ordinary Check expiry. Store the submission/consent time, inspected scope and purpose/notice version, reporting principal, fixed expiry and report identifier. Signed-in reports use the opaque Owner association; Guest reports use their own Guest-context association, never a fabricated Account. The private service may map an Owner to its Account for permitted administration but receives no diagnostic payload. This evidence is report-lifetime data, not a separate permanent archive.

## User management and Guest removal

Signed-in users can see their own report identifiers, receipt/deletion dates and handling states and withdraw a report in **both web and extension**, independent of Plan and History consent. The overview does not restore an expired Check or create another History view. Use current Account/Owner authorization under [ADR-0013](0013-authentication-roles.md); a report ID alone never authorizes a read or removal. No new transactional email is selected or added to [ADR-0019](0019-transactional-email-catalog.md)'s closed catalog.

Guests can report in either client environment without registration. On submission provide a random, report-specific removal code that the Guest can deliberately keep; do not persist Reference content on the device. Keep code entry and feedback accessible in DE/EN. The code authorizes **deletion of exactly that report**, not reading its content, recovering the original Check or taking over an Account. Do not put it in URLs, logs, telemetry, issue bodies or the private service's content-free administration.

Treat the code as a secret: store a verifier rather than plaintext, enforce request limits and action/resource binding, and consume/invalidate it with deletion. Its validity ends no later than the report's deadline. Use the normal current-authorized API entry and service-side safety checks; the verification API additionally validates the removal credential. The current Guest context need not be the original one for this deletion-only proof to work after the original Check/context expires. This is a narrowly scoped removal capability, not a bypass for ordinary Guest/Owner content reads or an exception to current authorization on those reads. Validate the actual transport and abuse controls before activation.

Without the code or still-valid original Guest authority, there is no automatic recovery from a report number. Separately qualified statutory-rights/support procedures remain available where applicable; they do not grant content access based on an unverified claim. Later sign-in does not transfer Guest reports, original Checks or removal authority to the Account. Removal does not require restoring erased Check data or retaining its original Guest authentication secret indefinitely.

## Fixed expiry and deletion precedence

Set the reported copy's maximum lifetime to **90 days from receipt**, subject to legal confirmation before activation. This is a separate reported-copy policy, not the paid History period or a new specification Initial value. Investigation, edits to handling state, Re-checks and Plan changes do not extend its deadline.

Delete the copy earlier when the user withdraws it, deliberately deletes its original Check, or deletes its associated Account/Owner. Ordinary automatic expiry of the original Check, including History expiry, does not itself delete the separately consented copy; it can remain until its own deadline. Do not interpret removal of one Reference from a Check, ordinary sign-out or loss of a page/panel as a new report-deletion command. Withdrawal affects the reported copy, not the original Check's independent retention or Quota.

Deletion takes effect immediately in live verification data, associated handling records, temporary material and pending report work. Consent evidence, handling notes and removal-credential verifier expire/delete **no later than the copy**, including earlier removal. Serialize deletion with submission, investigation updates and content delivery; an in-flight handler must not recreate or newly expose deleted material. A retry or duplicate delivery must not silently recreate a withdrawn copy or reset its deadline. The submitted snapshot remains immutable while it exists; immutability is not immunity from erasure.

Keep only necessary content-free deletion/audit metadata under the applicable existing schedules: ID-only tombstones for restore safety, [ADR-0015](0015-security-abuse-protection.md)'s 30-day security events and [ADR-0014](0014-operator-administration.md)'s 12-month administrative audit where the event actually belongs. Do not turn those into a second consent/content archive, indefinite Account association or a new blanket retention period. Legal/privacy review must confirm the actual minimum evidence and identifier allocation; a need for a different policy reopens the decision before activation.

Use [ADR-0012](0012-hosting-operations.md)'s existing bounded backup/deletion safeguards: no more than the existing 14-day backup tail, deletion/tombstone replay and expiry sweeps before restored traffic. Prove the reported copy and its credentials cannot become usable again after removal/expiry. The reported copy needs its own qualified encryption/retention class; retaining a pointer to the original transient Check's shredded key cannot support the separately permitted lifetime. This selects no new key store or indefinite recovery archive. Existing 24-hour transient-content protection is unchanged.

## Operator investigation and improvement

Only the separately granted reporting permission enables the future Operator browser to read a retained copy **directly from the verification API**. Enforce current permission and resource validity during reads/delivery; ordinary Operator administration and the private backend still cannot inspect verification content. Keep diagnostic notes and handling state in the verification program, separately from the immutable submission and under its same deadline. Private audit/case metadata stays content-free.

Closing a report changes neither the original Verdict/Manual override nor Quota and is not proof of a confirmed cause or independently reviewed ground truth. Do not copy report content into tracker issues, source/data repositories, email support archives or external analysis/AI services. A reporting action does not authorize provider disclosure, model training, product analytics, public corpus release or new remote diagnostics.

Preserve [ADR-0006](0006-reference-test-set-quality-gate.md): **never copy a reported wrong Verdict into any set or fixture suite**. The Operator may independently rebuild a case from public metadata of the affected Source, without the user's text or report payload; that new record enters the Development set first and follows existing provenance, rights, ground-truth review and set-separation rules. Do not convert a report's handling outcome into a fixture label. Outcome-changing improvements still require the existing Scoring rules/Measured configuration versioning and passed quality gate.

## Contractual return, rights and activation evidence

Explicitly assess reported copies under [ADR-0021](0021-online-dpa-workflow.md)'s agreement/processing relationship. Where covered by the applicable DPA, include still-retained copies in the customer's verified deletion instruction or return followed by deletion. The entitled recipient receives content directly from the verification program; the Operator coordinates scope/status without a private-backend payload relay. Neither investigation nor an open support/rights/return case extends deletion deadlines or permits backup resurrection.

Qualify actual roles, lawful bases, third-party rights, scope and authority rather than declaring all reporting to be either customer processing or an independent reuse purpose. The removal code is deletion-only evidence, not proof of contractual or data-subject access entitlement. Account deletion and blocked/deleted-account rights fulfilment retain their distinct existing safeguards; no agreement/invoice/company knowledge transfers report access. No separate error-analysis retention claim overrides a valid covered deletion instruction.

Before activating the should feature demonstrate:

- DE/EN, keyboard/screen-reader submission and actual payload inspection; no copy without explicit per-report consent; no document/other-Reference/filename leakage; exact result/override/version binding and fixed expiry.
- Both-client own-report management across Free/paid and History on/off; Guest submission/removal after Check/context expiry, without registration or Account migration; no content read through removal-code/report-ID guessing, replay or cross-Owner attempts.
- Current reporting permission, direct verification-side Operator reads, content-free service/audit boundaries, separate handling records and no result/Quota change on closure. Check every human/tool/export path against the no-secondary-content-archive rule.
- Withdrawal, deliberate Check deletion, Account/Owner deletion, natural Check expiry, concurrent submission/handling/delivery, retry, deadline and restore cases preserve deletion precedence, invalidate credentials and never recreate content. Qualify actual encryption/key lifetime, backup expiry and removal before traffic.
- Legal/privacy approval of purpose, actual lawful bases/rights, notice/consent evidence and its deletion, 90-day maximum, backup tail, identifiers and DPA/rights allocation. Demonstrate timely scoped return/deletion under unchanged deadlines; no impossible fulfilment promise or silent retention extension.

These are feature-activation evidence requirements, not claims of completed UI, runtime, legal, accessibility or security tests. Launch planning preserves the hook and documents these gates without collecting reports or bringing the should feature forward.
